About

I report on how technology moves between China, Africa and Europe. Journalist and researcher in Köln. I edit at Deutsche Welle and write about the rules that shape platforms, AI and the people who use them.

For eight years I've covered the tech industry in China, East Africa, the United States and Germany. The thread running through it is how technology flows between regions, and who gets to set the rules along the way.

My research looks at China-Africa tech flows and at EU digital regulation, including the Digital Services Act, the Digital Markets Act and the AI Act. My research and reporting on China-Africa tech has been referenced by the Alliance for Securing Democracy, the Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung, the United States Institute of Peace, the Africa Policy Research Institute and the Center for Global Studies in Bonn.
